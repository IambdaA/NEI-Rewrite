# NEI Rewrite: every feature, with checks (expect ...) that pass or fail by themselves. Runs unchanged on every version and
# loader: positions come from NEI itself (search, finditem, clickinv, clickpath, clickbottom, toolbar).
# Needs JEI for the fuel pages; sections marked (server) need NEI on the server, which singleplayer always has.
world
wait 20
cmd gamemode creative
cmd effect clear @s
cmd clear @s
cmd kill @e[type=item]
cmd time set 1000
cmd weather clear
inv
wait 40
screen
# a normal creative tab (the Search tab would take every key, as in vanilla), and an empty bookmark page (the
# instance's own bookmarks are backed up and restored by the runner)
clickwindow 13 -14
wait 10
expect screenkind == creative
call Bookmarks.grid().items.clear()
call Bookmarks.grid().changed()
cheatmode 2
call NEIConfig.QUANTITY.set(0)
call NEIConfig.HISTORY_ENABLED.set(true)
call NEIConfig.ZOOM_ENABLED.set(false)
call NEIConfig.SEARCH_HIDE_UNTIL.set(false)
call NEIConfig.RECIPE_INFINITE_SCROLL.set(false)
call NEIConfig.RECIPE_SHIFT_OVERLAY.set(false)
clickwindow 60 5
wait 5

section 27 autocraft the NTM liquidator helmet from its recipe chain (lead and rubber cladding, duct tape ...)
requires hbm:liquidator_helmet
close
wait 10
cmd gamemode creative
cmd clear @s
inv
wait 20
clickwindow 13 -14
wait 5
call Bookmarks.grid().items.clear()
call Bookmarks.grid().changed()
call NEIConfig.FAVORITES_ENABLED.set(true)
call NEIConfig.FAVORITE_GENERATE.set(true)
call NEIConfig.AUTOCRAFT.set(true)
search liquidator suit helmet
wait 30
finditem liquidator suit helmet
wait 3
key r
wait 10
movepath NEIClientEvents.overlay.heartHits.get(0).x NEIClientEvents.overlay.heartHits.get(0).y 4 4
wait 3
press shift
key a
release shift
wait 10
expect chaingroups == 1
expect chainout:hbm:liquidator_helmet == 1
expect chaincraft:hbm:cladding_lead == 2
expect chainin:hbm:cladding_lead == 0
expect chaincraft:hbm:ducttape >= 10
expect chainin:hbm:ducttape == 0
probe chain
key esc
wait 5
close
wait 10
cmd gamemode survival
cmd clear @s
cmd tp @s ~ ~ ~ 0 0
cmd setblock ~ ~1 ~2 minecraft:crafting_table
wait 10
use
wait 20
expect screenkind == crafting
givemissing
wait 20
expect chain contains missing={}
finditem BOOKMARKS liquidator suit helmet
wait 3
press shift
key c
release shift
waituntil inv:hbm:liquidator_helmet 1 120
expect inv:hbm:liquidator_helmet == 1
shot s27-helmet-crafted
close
wait 10
cmd setblock ~ ~1 ~2 minecraft:air
cmd gamemode creative
